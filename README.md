# NextCRM

An independently developed Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform.

## Project Overview

NextCRM aims to provide a centralized platform for managing customer relationships, sales activities, business operations, and project workflows.

## Planned Features

- User Authentication and Role Management
- CRM Dashboard
- Account and Contact Management
- Lead Management
- Opportunity Tracking
- Tasks and Activities
- Project Management
- Invoice Management
- Reports and Analytics
